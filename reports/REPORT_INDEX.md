## 6. Deviations, unexpected findings and limits

Post-scoring changes are logged in `DEVIATIONS.md` (created
in the 2026-11-20 review round): entry 1, the Moirai 2.0
release-date correction; entry 2, the Granite TTM context
re-run. Every other post-plan
change was recorded as a numbered decision in `DECISIONS.md`
instead. The material items:

- **Target-span alignment (DECISIONS #36).** The first
  adapter generation produced forecasts from the context end,
  but each target span starts 15–21 hours after the context
  end (the next local day). The v1 full runs for Moirai-MoE
  and TiRex were therefore misaligned (scoring about −115%
  and −123%) and were **discarded**; all ten adapters were
  corrected to generate through the last target hour and
  slice, and the affected models were re-run. The corrected
  Moirai 2.0 score (+37.2% day) verifies the fix.
- **Adapter findings.** Sundial runs locally on CPU (#35).
  Toto needed a paired torchvision and a full-length id_mask
  (#37). Granite TTM returns point forecasts only — its
  checkpoint trains with an MSE loss and has no
  distribution-head weights; a first merged run recorded
  degenerate intervals with coverage 0 and was discarded
  (#38). Sundial's 80% intervals cover 0.321 (day) and 0.325
  (week) of outcomes in the scored parquet — a property of
  the model's intervals as shipped, verified in the data,
  not an adapter defect. A review-round check (2026-11-20)
  ran the model's official quick-start generate call next to
  the adapter on identical contexts with the same seed: the
  quantiles were identical at every level, and the stored
  widths sit within sampling variation of a fresh draw, so
  the low coverage is the model's own — its intervals are
  the empirical ranges of 20 samples, as shipped
  (DECISIONS #51).
- **Granite TTM context (DECISIONS #52, DEVIATIONS.md
  entry 2).** The v1 run used the checkpoint's 512-hour
  default revision. Re-run at the 1,536-hour revision in the
  review round: day +25.1 → +26.3, week +17.0 → +15.9; the
  model's rank is unchanged and the 1,536-hour version is
  the scored one.
- **Time-MoE (DECISIONS #34, #39, #45).** Unscored in v1, as
  recorded in sections 3 and 5. The local re-run's two
  completed series (AECI: 3,381 s; AVA: 825 s) imply a pace
  far above the original ~15 h estimate.
- **Step 8.** Completed on 2026-11-20 after two blocked
  attempts (an egress-path timeout, then a storage-host
  permission approval that only the account holder could
  grant). The dataset stands at version 2, verified private,
  and the rebuild notebook is pushed, private, with its
  first run complete. Full record: DECISIONS #44, #46 and
  #47, and section 2 above.
- **Live track.** Committed disabled pending publication and
  the `EIA_API_KEY` secret (brief defaults). The EIA API
  client is tested against a fixture written to the
  documented v2 schema; the fixture must be re-recorded from
  the live API once the key exists (DECISIONS #41).
- **Limits.** Scores are one retrospective window on one
  domain; intervals are paired-bootstrap intervals on that
  window. The operator forecast's own issue time is as
  published in EIA-930 and is scored on the same target
  hours. Seven windows had no reported actuals and are
  excluded for all methods.
